Data Analytés — Member Commitment

By joining Data Analytés, you are not just accessing a curriculum — you are joining a family built on trust, humility, and shared growth. This agreement defines what that means in practice.

01
I will not share group resources outside the group. The curriculum, links, documents, and materials shared within Data Analytés are for members only. Sharing them with non-members without the coordinator's approval is a violation of this agreement and grounds for removal.
02
I will show up with humility. I am not here to prove I am the smartest. I am here to grow. I will not belittle, talk down to, or compete against fellow members.
03
I will take every activity seriously. When I commit to an activity, a session, a task, or a project, I will follow through. Consistency is a form of respect — for the group and for myself.
04
I will contribute what I know. I will not hoard knowledge. If I learn something that can help the group, I will share it. We rise together or not at all.
05
I will not discriminate. I will not treat any member differently on the basis of gender, background, level of prior knowledge, or any other reason. Everyone in this group deserves equal respect.
06
I see this group as a family. This is not a competition. This is not a leaderboard. This is a community of people investing in each other. I will protect that.
07
When I succeed, I will come back. Once I have established myself professionally, I commit to returning to mentor the next generation of Data Analytés members. The legacy is built by everyone.

Our Philosophy
What Data Analytés stands for — and why it matters.

The Data Analytés Philosophy

"We are not here to outshine each other — we are here to grow together. Every member brings something to the table, and every contribution matters. We learn, we share, we build, and we rise as one."

◈
Humility over ego. You don't need to prove you're the smartest. You need to be willing to learn, unlearn, and relearn. The most dangerous person in a growth community is the one who thinks they already know enough.
◈
Commitment over convenience. Every activity, every session, every task is taken seriously. Showing up halfway is not showing up. If you are here, be fully here.
◈
Team over individual. Your success is tied to the group's success. We don't compete within — we compete with the outside world together. When one member wins, the group wins.
◈
Openness over gatekeeping. Whether you have never touched a dataset or you've been coding for years — you belong here if you're willing to grow. What you know, you share. No hoarding of information.
◈
Legacy over short-term gain. This group is designed to outlive your time in it. When you leave, you come back as a mentor. You bring new people. You protect what was built. That is how Data Analytés grows beyond us.
◈
Family over competition. This is not a leaderboard. This is a family. We protect, support, and invest in each other. The person who struggles this month may be the one who helps you next year. Treat everyone accordingly.
